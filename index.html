Privacy Policy for Stack It!

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

ShadowByte Labs provides the Stack It! mobile game as a free-to-play application. This document explains our data collection, usage, and disclosure practices for anyone playing our game.

1. Data Collection and Usage

To provide and improve our game, we might need you to share specific operational data. Any information we request is kept secure and handled strictly according to this policy.

Stack It! utilizes third-party tools that may independently gather identifying information. Please review the privacy guidelines of these external service providers:

  • Google Play Services
  • Unity

2. Error Logs and Diagnostic Data

If Stack It! crashes or encounters a critical error during gameplay, diagnostic data is collected via third-party systems like Unity Diagnostics and the Google Play Console. These crash reports (Log Data) contain technical details such as your device model, operating system version, IP address, active game settings, and timestamps. This information is strictly utilized to troubleshoot bugs, prevent future crashes, and enhance overall game performance.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our application does not explicitly utilize web cookies. However, the third-party frameworks and libraries integrated into the app might employ tracking technologies to optimize their operations. You can configure your device or network settings to block these identifiers, though doing so may impact certain game features.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with external companies to host our services, analyze gameplay metrics, and manage backend infrastructure. These authorized partners may process your data solely to carry out operational tasks for ShadowByte Labs. They are strictly prohibited from sharing your information or using it for their own unrelated purposes.

5. Data Security

Protecting your information is highly important to us, and we implement industry-standard digital safeguards to secure it. However, please note that no electronic transmission over the internet or digital storage system is completely foolproof, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security against all unauthorized access.

6. Children's Privacy

Stack It! is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not intentionally gather personally identifiable information from users in this demographic. If we discover that a child under 13 has transmitted personal data to our servers, we will promptly delete it. Parents or guardians who believe their child has shared sensitive information with us should contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

7. Policy Updates

ShadowByte Labs reserves the right to modify this document at any time to reflect changing technical or legal requirements. We recommend reviewing this page periodically. Any updates become legally effective the moment they are published on this webpage.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us directly at: shadowbytelabsapp@gmail.com.
